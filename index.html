About个人简介

Prof. Wen's research focuses on intelligent manufacturing technologies that integrate advanced materials, precision equipment, optical sensing, robotics, and artificial intelligence. His group designs and builds one-of-a-kind manufacturing systems and functional materials to enable high-precision fabrication, online process monitoring, and closed-loop control for applications in advanced optics, electronics, biomedicine, aerospace, and space manufacturing.

温教授的研究聚焦于智能制造技术，融合先进材料、精密装备、光学传感、机器人与人工智能。其团队设计并搭建独一无二的制造系统与功能材料，实现高精度加工、在线过程监测与闭环控制，应用于先进光学、电子、生物医学、航空航天及太空制造等领域。

Research Highlights & Technology Translation科研亮点与技术转化

High-resolution glass 3D printing and glass micro/nano manufacturing高分辨率玻璃 3D 打印与玻璃微纳制造 World-leading fused silica glass 3D printing and micro/nano fabrication for micro-optics, photonics, microfluidics, mechanical metamaterials, biomedical devices, and semiconductor applications.面向微光学、光子学、微流控、力学超材料、生物医学器件与半导体应用，发展国际领先的熔融石英玻璃 3D 打印与微纳加工技术。
Licensed technology — 3D-printed silica glass with sub-micron features技术许可 — 亚微米特征 3D 打印石英玻璃 Inventor of a 3D-printed silica glass technology covered by a licensing agreement registered in May 2025, transferring sub-micron glass printing capability to industry.作为发明人的 3D 打印石英玻璃技术已于 2025 年 5 月完成许可登记，将亚微米玻璃打印能力向产业转移。
Intelligent in-situ laser process monitoring — IntraSpect™智能原位激光过程监测 — IntraSpect™ Intelligent optical sensing and AI-enabled monitoring technology for real-time laser melt pool inspection and quality control in precision welding and advanced manufacturing.面向精密焊接与先进制造的智能光学传感与 AI 监测技术，实现激光熔池实时检测与质量控制。
Space manufacturing and commercial space technologies太空制造与商业航天技术 In-orbit manufacturing, space assembly, lightweight structures, robotic manufacturing, and space optical fibre payloads through the PolyU–CSU Joint Research Centre (RCSMT).依托理大–中科院联合研究中心（RCSMT），开展在轨制造、空间装配、轻量化结构、机器人制造与空间光纤载荷研究。
Cross-border technology transfer and innovation policy service跨境技术转移与创新政策服务 Contribution to regional innovation initiatives and technology transfer platforms, including Su-Gang Li-Zhi, a Jiangsu Province-approved International Cooperation Key Technology Transfer Center under the PolyU Wuxi Research Institute.参与区域创新与技术转移平台建设，包括江苏省认定的国际合作重点技术转移中心「苏港理智」（理大无锡研究院）。
